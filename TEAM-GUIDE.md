## 1 · Your daily 20 minutes
1. Pick your name (top right) so your sends are logged for the team.
2. Toggle **Unsent only**, work top to bottom.
3. On a card: read the draft → **✉ Open in Gmail** (ready draft, mockup attached) → glance → **Send**.
4. Flip the **Sent** switch. Everyone sees it live — nobody double-touches a lead.

## 2 · Follow-ups (Relances) — most replies come from the chase
Each card has a **Relances · J+3 · J+10 · J+17** panel with three ready messages.
- No reply ~3 days → send **J+3**. Day 10 → **J+10**. Day 17 → **J+17** (graceful exit).
- Each has its own **✉ Gmail** + **Copy**. Warm, never pushy. **Stop the moment they reply.**

## 3 · When a lead REPLIES — this is where the money is
A positive reply is the trigger. Tell João, or run it in Claude Code:
1. **Build their real preview** — `/meridante-preview <company>` → full Kiné-level multi-page site, deployed live, + design PDF + page images.
2. **Send the preview email** — the card flips to **REPLIED** with the delivery email ready; it offers a 15-min call.
3. **Close it** — `/meridante-close <company>` → preps the call, builds the 3-tier proposal PDF, drafts the close email with deposit + booking links.
